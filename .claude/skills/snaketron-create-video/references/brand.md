# SnakeTron trailer brand

Build an editorial arcade-sport identity: bright arena geometry against graphite, hard-edged broadcast typography, and quick pixel-slice motion.

## Palette

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Graphite | `#3F3F41` |
| Arena blue | `#3B82F6` |
| Knockout red | `#EF4444` |
| Celebration coral | `#F6707B` |
| Trophy gold | `#F8C84A` |

## Type and layout

- Set heroes in Barlow Condensed ExtraBold Italic, uppercase.
- Set utility copy in Inter.
- Slant compositions forward. Use hard 2–4 px rules, cropped numerals, and 8 px graphite/black shadows.
- Use white as the primary title color, gold for stakes, blue for energy, and red/coral only for impacts and celebrations.
- Preserve mobile legibility: keep critical type inside the center 70% and preview at 640×360.

## Motion and sound

- Enter with short slice, wipe, or pixelize transitions; reserve fadeblack for chapter breaks.
- Cut on musical beats. Land eliminations with one dry impact plus a brief RGB split and decaying shake.
- Support boosts with a rising whoosh; support banks with a bright confirmation chime.
- Target `-14 LUFS` integrated and `-1.5 dBTP`; duck music beneath SFX instead of simply raising SFX gain.

Use `assets/identity/snaketron-wordmark.svg` for browser title-card captures, `assets/lut/snaketron-identity.cube` for conservative finishing, and the bundled original SFX. Check `assets/LICENSES.json` before distribution.
